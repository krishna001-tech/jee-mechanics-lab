🧠 Why is the time of flight different?

When a projectile is launched from a height, it does not land at the same vertical level from which it was launched.

y = h + u sinθ · t − ½gt²

At the ground, y = 0. Therefore the positive solution is:

t = [u sinθ + √((u sinθ)² + 2gh)] / g

💡 Increasing the launch height gives the projectile more time before it reaches the ground.
