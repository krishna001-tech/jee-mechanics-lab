Projectile motion is the two-dimensional motion of an object launched into the air under the influence of gravity.

The horizontal and vertical motions can be treated independently. Horizontal acceleration is zero while vertical acceleration is equal to g downward.

The initial velocity can be resolved into two components: Vx = V₀ cos θ and Vy = V₀ sin θ.

📚 Important Formulae

Time of flight: T = [u sinθ + √((u sinθ)² + 2gh)] / g
Maximum height: H = h + (u² sin²θ)/(2g)
Horizontal range: R = u cosθ × T

An inclined plane is a surface making an angle θ with the horizontal. The weight of the block acts vertically downward.

The weight can be resolved into two components: mg sinθ along the plane and mg cosθ perpendicular to the plane.

Static friction prevents motion until the component of gravity along the plane exceeds the maximum available static friction.

Once the block starts moving, kinetic friction acts opposite to the direction of motion.

📚 Important Formulae

Weight: W = mg
Normal force: N = mg cosθ
Parallel component: W∥ = mg sinθ
Maximum static friction: fₛ(max) = μₛN
Kinetic friction: fₖ = μₖN
Acceleration while sliding: a = g(sinθ − μₖcosθ)
Angle of repose: tanθ = μₛ
