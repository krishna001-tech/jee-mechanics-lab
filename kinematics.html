Projectile Motion

🧠 Why is vertical velocity zero?

As the projectile moves upward, gravity continuously decreases its vertical velocity.

vy = u sinθ − gt

At maximum height, the projectile stops moving upward for an instant.

vy = 0

Therefore:

0 = u sinθ − gt
tmax = u sinθ / g

💡 Remember: At maximum height, vertical velocity = 0, but horizontal velocity is still present.
