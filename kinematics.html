Projectile motion is the motion of an object launched into the air under the influence of gravity.

The horizontal and vertical motions can be studied independently.

JEE Mains & Advanced — Sample Questions

JEE Main (conceptual / short)

1. A ball is projected at 30 m/s at 60° above the horizontal. Ignore air resistance. What is the horizontal range?

Range = (v^2 sin 2θ)/g. Here v=30, θ=60°, sin120°=sin60°=√3/2. Range = (900 * √3/2) / 9.8 ≈ (900*0.866)/9.8 ≈ 779.4 / 9.8 ≈ 79.53 m.

2. A projectile is launched from a height h above ground. How does initial height affect time of flight?

Increasing initial height increases time of flight. Solve y(t)=h+v_y t - (1/2) g t^2 = 0 for positive root. Time grows with √h for fixed v_y.

3. For a fixed speed, at what two angles will a projectile have the same range?

If θ gives a range R, then (90°−θ) gives the same range because sin(2θ)=sin(180°−2θ).

JEE Advanced (challenging)

1. A particle is projected from point A on a plane inclined at angle α with speed u at angle β above the incline. Find the range along the incline.

Resolve components along and perpendicular to plane and use motion equations with g component g cosα along normal and g sinα along plane. Range formula (along incline) = [2 u^2 cosβ sin(β−α)] / [g cos^2 α]. (Derivation omitted — use coordinate rotation.)

2. A projectile of speed v is launched from ground at angle θ. A target at distance R and height H must be hit. Derive condition relating v, θ, R, H.

Use parametric equations x=v cosθ t, y=v sinθ t - 1/2 g t^2. Solve for t=R/(v cosθ) and substitute to get H = R tanθ - (g R^2)/(2 v^2 cos^2 θ). Rearranged gives condition for θ existence.

3. A projectile passes through two points at the same level separated by distance d. Show that the highest point is at midpoint horizontally.

Symmetry of parabolic trajectory: horizontal motion uniform, vertical symmetric about apex. For equal heights, times t1 and t2 satisfy t1+t2 = time of flight; midpoint in time corresponds to midpoint in x.
