🧠 Why does the projectile reach maximum height?

At the highest point of the projectile, its vertical velocity becomes zero.

vᵧ = u sinθ − gt

At maximum height:

vᵧ = 0

Therefore:

t = u sinθ / g

The projectile keeps moving horizontally because its horizontal velocity remains constant (when air resistance is ignored).
